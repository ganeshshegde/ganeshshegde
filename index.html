Why integrated matters

Most cashew you buy has changed hands five times. Ours changes hands once.

A split supply chain hides problems: unknown crop years get blended, moisture drifts in transit, and nobody owns the defect. Owning every stage means the person who graded the kernel can still name the orchard it came from.

One chain of custody

Every carton carries a lot code that resolves back to the collection centre, the procurement week, the cooker batch and the grading shift. Recall scope is hours of production, not a season.

Food safety we can prove

Metal detection and X-ray on the finished line, swab schedules on every contact surface, and a retained sample from each lot held for the full shelf life. Audit trails are exportable, not anecdotal.

Nothing leaves as waste

The shell gives up CNSL and briquette-grade cake; the testa goes to tannin extraction. By-product revenue is what lets us pay farmers above the mandi rate and still quote competitively.

The integrated value chain

Nine stages. All of them ours.

From the moment raw cashew nut is weighed at a collection centre to the moment a nitrogen-flushed pack is sealed, the nut never leaves our custody.

  1. 01

    Farm-gate procurement

    Direct buying at 40 collection centres across the Western Ghats belt, plus contracted imports of West African and Indonesian RCN to hold the line through the off-season. Nut count, outturn and moisture are recorded at intake.

    Checked: nut count/kg · outturn · moisture · foreign matter

  2. 02

    Conditioning & storage

    Raw nut is cleaned, de-stoned and sized, then rested in humidity-controlled silos at 8–9% moisture. Sizing before cooking is what keeps the cutter set consistent and the breakage rate low.

    Capacity: 6,000 MT of conditioned raw nut

  3. 03

    Steam cooking

    Saturated steam at controlled pressure for a short, exact hold — long enough to make the shell brittle and free the kernel, short enough to leave the kernel's colour and bite untouched. Cooker batches are logged with time, pressure and lot.

    Boilers run on our own cashew-shell briquettes

  4. 04

    Shelling

    Automated cutter banks matched to each size grade, with hand-shelling retained for the largest counts where a machine still costs you whole kernels. Shell and kernel separate immediately — shell to the CNSL plant, kernel onward.

    Whole-kernel recovery: 94% average

  5. 05

    Drying (borma)

    Hot-air drying draws kernel moisture down so the testa lifts cleanly and the kernel hardens against handling damage. Under-dry and the skin tears the kernel; over-dry and it shatters in the peeler.

    Target: 3% kernel moisture at peeling

  6. 06

    Peeling

    Pneumatic peeling on the bulk stream, hand-finishing on premium counts. No caustic, no bleach, no oil — the kernel that reaches grading is exactly the kernel that came out of the shell.

    Testa recovered for tannin extraction

  7. 07

    Grading & sorting

    Optical sorters pull colour and defect, calibrated sieves set the count, and trained graders make the final call on every premium lot. Grades are built to AFI specification and verified against retained standards.

    W180 · W210 · W240 · W320 · W450 · SW · splits · pieces

  8. 08

    Value addition

    Roasting, salting and seasoning on a dedicated allergen-controlled line: sea salt, black pepper, peri-peri, honey-roast and custom profiles developed with the buyer. Private label from 500 kg per SKU.

    Own-label and white-label runs both supported

  9. 09

    Packing & dispatch

    Vita-pack tins with nitrogen and CO2 flush for bulk export, vacuum pouches for retail, flexible consumer formats for brand work. Metal detection and X-ray sit after packing, not before.

    FCL, LCL and air freight · 22.68 kg tins, 10/25 kg cartons, retail packs

Sourcing & sustainability

The farm gate is part of the factory

We buy from 4,200 farmer families across Uttara Kannada, Dakshina Kannada and the Konkan coast, most of them working under five acres. Paying at the collection centre on the same day, in full, is not a CSR line item — it is what keeps the good lots coming to us instead of to a broker.

Same-day payment at the centre Above-mandi price for graded lots Free grafted saplings Agronomy visits each season

On our side of the gate: shell briquettes fire the boilers, a rooftop solar array covers a meaningful share of daytime load, process water is treated and reused for the orchards, and the unit sends no process waste to landfill.
